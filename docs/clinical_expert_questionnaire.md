# Phenotype-specific progression in Parkinson's disease: expert questionnaire (round 1)

> Version 0.1, 2 November 2026. Round 1 of a two-round questionnaire for the study "Phenotype-specific progression mechanisms in Parkinson's disease" (protocol draft v0.3). Please return it by [date] to [investigator name, email].

## About this questionnaire

**Purpose.** We will study whether Parkinson's phenotypes, such as tremor-dominant versus postural instability and gait difficulty (PIGD), get worse through different pathways, or through the same pathways at different speeds. The data are the yearly assessments of the Parkinson's Progression Markers Initiative (PPMI). Before anyone looks at phenotype-specific data, clinical experts decide four things: how phenotypes are defined, which symptom domains matter, which domains plausibly drive which, and which differences between phenotypes the study should test.

**Time.** About 45 to 60 minutes. Please answer on your own, without discussing with other panel members, from your clinical knowledge and experience. There are no right answers, and "unsure" is a useful answer. You may skip any question.

**How your answers are used.** Answers are combined across experts. In round 2 you will see the group's anonymised answers on the items where experts disagreed, and you can revise yours. The combined answers fix the study's phenotype definitions, its progression map and its pre-registered hypotheses. No answer is reported with a name. Participation is voluntary.

## Glossary

- **Phenotype:** a clinical type of Parkinson's disease recognisable at diagnosis, such as tremor-dominant or PIGD.
- **Domain:** a group of symptoms measured on one scale, such as cognition (MoCA).
- **Direct effect:** an effect of one domain on another that does not pass only through a third domain in the list.
- **Same route, faster:** the same domains drive decline in both phenotypes, but decline is quicker in one.
- **Different route:** in one phenotype, other domains drive decline, or the same domains drive it more or less strongly.
- **Confirmatory hypothesis:** a difference fixed before the analysis and formally tested; the study keeps about ten.
- **PPMI:** the Parkinson's Progression Markers Initiative, an international cohort that assesses people with Parkinson's disease every year.
