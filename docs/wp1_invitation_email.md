# WP1: invitation to the expert panel (draft)

> Draft of 4 November 2026, for the investigator to adapt and send. Fill in everything in [brackets]. Attach `docs/Clinical_Expert_Questionnaire_Round1_v0.2.docx`, rebuilt after its return date and contact are filled in (runbook, §3).

## Before sending

- **Ethics.** Ask your institution whether an expert survey needs ethics review or an exemption (protocol §14), and put the outcome in the email.
- **Who.** At least five raters, ideally movement-disorder neurologists. With three or four, the map is described as investigator-led and its analyses are repeated without the disputed links (D25).
- **Tracking.** Save each returned questionnaire in `data/wp1/round1/` (git-ignored, because the files name the raters). Send a reminder after two weeks.

## The email

**Subject:** Invitation to an expert panel: do Parkinson's phenotypes progress through different pathways? (about one hour, two rounds)

Dear Dr [surname],

I am [your name], a PhD candidate at [institution], working with [supervisor]. I would like to invite you to join a small expert panel for a study of how Parkinson's disease progresses in different motor phenotypes.

The study uses the yearly assessments of the Parkinson's Progression Markers Initiative (PPMI). It asks whether tremor-dominant and PIGD patients get worse through different pathways, or through the same pathways at different speeds: for example, whether gait problems drive the loss of independence more strongly in one phenotype. Before anyone analyses the phenotypes' data, clinical experts decide how phenotypes are defined, which symptom domains matter, and which domains plausibly drive which. The panel's answers fix these choices in the pre-registered protocol.

**What it involves:**
- **Round 1:** the attached questionnaire, about 45 to 60 minutes, by [date].
- **Round 2,** about four weeks later: about 20 minutes. You revisit only the items where the panel disagreed, next to the group's anonymised answers.
- **Anonymity:** answers are combined across experts and never reported with your name. You may skip any question, and taking part is voluntary.
- **Ethics:** [approved by / exempt from review by ..., reference ...].
- **Recognition:** panel members can be acknowledged, or be co-authors if their contribution meets authorship criteria (question I3).

To answer, type into the Word file and return it to me by email. If it would help, I am happy to go through it with you in a short call.

Thank you for considering this. Please let me know if you have any questions.

Kind regards,
[Your name]
[Title, institution, email, phone]
